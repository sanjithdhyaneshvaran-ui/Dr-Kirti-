Diagnosis

Triple Assessment

3 min read · Medically reviewed by Dr. Kirti

Triple assessment is the gold-standard way to find out what a breast change is — whether it is cancer or, far more often, a benign condition. It combines three checks, usually in a single outpatient visit, with no fasting and no blood tests needed beforehand.

Why three checks are better than one

No single test is perfect. An examination, a scan and a each show something different. Put together, they give a highly accurate answer.

is used both to diagnose or rule out cancer and to confirm conditions, so you can stop worrying sooner.

The three parts of triple assessment

A clear answer
  1. 1 Clinical examinationDr. Kirti asks about your symptoms and history, then carefully examines both breasts and both armpits.
  2. 2 ImagingA mammogram, an ultrasound, or both, chosen for your age and what the examination shows.About mammograms →About breast ultrasound →
  3. 3 Needle biopsy, if neededIf anything on the scan needs a closer look, a small sample is taken with a needle, guided by ultrasound or mammogram.About breast biopsy →

What happens on the day

  3. Consultation With Dr. Kirti Consultation and examination with Dr. Kirti.
  4. Imaging Usually the same day Imaging, usually the same day.
  5. Biopsy, only if needed Around 15 to 30 minutes Under local anaesthetic. It takes around 15 to 30 minutes.
  6. Going home The same day You can go home the same day and return to most normal activities.

Getting your results

If the examination and scans are clearly normal or benign, you may get your answer on the day.

If you had a biopsy, the sample is examined in a laboratory. Results usually take a few working days, and Dr. Kirti will go through them with you in person.

If the three checks don't agree, Dr. Kirti will always look further, with a repeat biopsy or another test, until there is a clear answer.

Questions to ask Dr. Kirti

You may find it helpful to bring these to your appointment.

  2. 2.Will I need a biopsy today?
  3. 3.When and how will I get my results?
  4. 4.Is there anything I should avoid after the biopsy?
