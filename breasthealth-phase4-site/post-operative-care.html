Recovering After Breast Surgery

3 min read · Medically reviewed by Dr. Kirti

Most women recover well from breast surgery within a few weeks. This guide explains how to look after your wound, manage s, get your arm moving again and know when to call us.

Looking after your wound

  • Keep the dressing clean and dry until you are told it can come off
  • Most stitches dissolve on their own and don't need removing
  • Wear a soft, supportive bra day and night for the first few weeks
  • Mild bruising, swelling and numbness around the scar are normal and improve over time

If you go home with a drain

A thin tube may be left in to drain fluid from the wound. It is usually removed after a few days to a week, once the fluid slows down.

Keep the bottle below the wound, note how much fluid collects each day if asked, and keep the area dry. Sometimes fluid collects after the drain is out (a ). It is harmless and can be drained in the clinic if needed.

Getting your arm moving

Gentle exercises help prevent stiffness and get back full movement. Start them as advised, usually from the day after surgery, and stop if anything hurts.

Arm exercises after breast surgery · Dr. Kirti Katherine Kabeer
1Shoulder shrugs and circles Lift and roll your shoulders gently.
2Wall climb Walk your fingers up a wall, a little higher each day.
3Hands behind head Lie down, clasp your hands behind your head, and let your elbows fall gently outwards.
4Arm raises Lift your arm forward and up, as far as is comfortable.

Getting back to daily life

ShoweringUsually after 48 hours, once advised; pat the wound dry
DrivingWhen you can turn the wheel and brake comfortably, usually after 2 to 3 weeks
WorkLight work after 2 to 4 weeks; longer for physical jobs or after reconstruction
LiftingAvoid heavy lifting for about 4 to 6 weeks
ExerciseWalking from the first days; other exercise after 4 to 6 weeks

When to call us

Call us if you have:

  • A fever, or feel shivery and unwell
  • Redness, heat or increasing pain around the wound
  • Swelling that is getting bigger
  • Bleeding or fluid leaking through the dressing

Recovery: FAQs

Most women have mild to moderate discomfort, well controlled with simple pain relief.

Small nerves may be affected during surgery. Numbness usually improves over months, but may not go away completely.

The tissue results are usually ready by your first follow-up, about 1 to 2 weeks after surgery.
