Know your doctor

Dr. Kirti Katherine Kabeer

Dr. Kirti is a breast surgeon with super-speciality training in oncoplastic breast surgery from the University of East Anglia, UK, and a fellowship in breast oncoplasty from the Royal College of Surgeons.

She cares for women with every kind of breast concern — from benign conditions like pain, cysts and infection, to screening, diagnosis and the surgical treatment of breast cancer — combining cancer surgery with techniques that preserve the natural shape of the breast.
